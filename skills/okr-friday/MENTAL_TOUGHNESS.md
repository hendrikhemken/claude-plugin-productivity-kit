# Mental Toughness Framework
*Based on Alex Hormozi's Model*

## What is Mental Toughness?

**Definition:** The likelihood that a bad thing changes how you act in a way that's against your goals.

**Key Insight:** Mental toughness is NOT binary (you have it or you don't) - it's a **skill you can develop** with 4 measurable components.

---

## The 4 Components

### 1. Tolerance
**"How much hard stuff can you take before you crack?"**
- How much hardship (or how long) you can endure before your behavior changes
- High tolerance: Long fuse - takes a LOT to throw you off
- Low tolerance: Short fuse - small things set you off

### 2. Fortitude
**"How low do you go when you DO crack?"**
- The intensity of behavior change once your tolerance threshold is surpassed
- High fortitude: Small change (barely noticeable to others)
- Low fortitude: Massive change (quit, lash out, spiral hard)

### 3. Resilience
**"How fast do you bounce back?"**
- How long it takes to return to normal behavior after acting out
- High resilience: Minutes or hours (bounce back fast!)
- Low resilience: Days, weeks, months
- Often the EASIEST to improve and has HUGE impact!

### 4. Adaptability
**"Do you get better or worse from hard times?"**
- Your new baseline compared to old baseline after a bad thing
- High: Stabilize ABOVE (got stronger from it!)
- Medium: Return to same baseline
- Low: Stabilize BELOW (permanently worse off)

## Quick Self-Check (For Weekly OKR Check-ins)

### Monday: Anticipation
**"What might test my mental toughness this week?"**
- Tough client call? Financial pressure? Deadline stress? Rejection risk?
- Awareness = preparation

### Friday: Reflection
**"What DID test my mental toughness this week?"**

If something happened, rate yourself 1-10 on each:
1. **Tolerance (1-10):** How long did you stay normal before behavior changed?
2. **Fortitude (1-10):** How small was the behavior change?
3. **Resilience (1-10):** How fast did you bounce back?
4. **Adaptability (1-10):** Are you better or worse now?

---

## When Bad Things Happen

1. **Recognize:** "Am I acting differently than I normally would?"
2. **Break the link:** Feelings ≠ actions. You can feel upset WITHOUT acting upset.
3. **Reverse course:** Full 180 - "What would I normally do right now?"
4. **Reframe:** "How can this bad thing serve me?"
